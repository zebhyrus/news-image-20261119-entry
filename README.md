A newspaper front page that sums up Thursday 19 November 2026 in one
image: the first Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral meeting in Shenzhen, the
ICJ judgment in the Rohingya genocide case, Anthropic's record Nasdaq
debut, Cyclone Akvan nearing India's east coast, the launch of GTA 6,
and Pew's projection that Islam will pass Christianity as the world's
largest religion by 2035.

The page is drawn as `entry.svg` and rendered to `entry.png`
(1024×1024). `main.ts` copies that image to the output directory.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This writes `out/entry.png`, identical to `entry.png`.

## Sources

Reuters and Yonhap on the Shenzhen summit; the ICJ and Foley Hoag on
the Rohingya judgment; Reuters and PYMNTS on the Anthropic IPO; the
India Meteorological Department on Cyclone Akvan; Rockstar and the BBC
on GTA 6; the Pew Research Center on the 2035 projection.
