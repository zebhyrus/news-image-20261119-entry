A one-page newspaper front page that sums up Thursday 19 November 2026 in
six stories, drawn as a hand-authored SVG and rendered to a 1024x1024 PNG.

The front page leads with the first-ever trilateral summit of Trump, Xi and
Putin at the APEC meeting in Shenzhen, then covers the third strike on the
Crimean Bridge this week, the widening US-Iran strikes across the Gulf,
the worldwide launch of Grand Theft Auto VI, Israeli tanks crossing the
Gaza ceasefire line, and a Van Gogh painting selling for a record
$190.4 million.

## The six stories

1. **Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen** — first-ever trilateral summit
   at APEC; Xi hugged Trump on the tarmac; nuclear arms control, Ukraine
   and the Iran conflict on the agenda.
2. **Crimean Bridge hit a third time** — Ukraine struck the Kerch Bridge
   again overnight; traffic halted, the railway hit, a 227-metre road
   section destroyed on 17 November.
3. **US-Iran Gulf strikes widen** — Iran fired at a US base in Kuwait and
   struck Bahrain again; the US hit a Revolutionary Guard missile site on
   Qeshm Island; a tanker struck in the Strait of Hormuz.
4. **GTA VI launches today** — back to Vice City on PlayStation 5 and
   Xbox Series X|S, $79.99; the most pre-ordered game in PlayStation
   Store history; 212 GB download.
5. **Israeli tanks cross Gaza ceasefire line** — a rare ground incursion;
   Australia, Canada, Italy and the UK jointly called on Israel to let
   aid reach flooded Gaza.
6. **Van Gogh sells for record $190.4M** — after a row over its Nazi
   history.

## Files

- `entry.svg` — the front page, drawn by hand in SVG
- `entry.png` — the rendered 1024x1024 image (190,472 bytes)
- `main.ts` — copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This writes `out/entry.png`, byte-identical to `entry.png` in this repo.
